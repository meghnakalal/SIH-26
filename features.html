💡 Frontline Digital Innovations

Core Features & Innovations

Engineered around the daily challenges of rural patients and ASHA community workers: confirmed slot booking, clinical risk triage, trackable e-referrals, live drug inventory lookups, and structured post-consultation care plans.

🔔 Notification
Feature 01 • Consultation Access

Appointment & Slot Booking

Eliminate unpredictable lines at Primary Health Centres. Frontline workers can pre-book confirmed slots with local Medical Officers or teleconsultation physicians.

“Appointment and slot visibility can help patients avoid unnecessary waiting and help ASHA workers plan visits.”

Feature 02 • Intelligent Triage

Risk-Based Earlier Consultation

Clinical priority algorithms assess vital signs recorded by ASHA workers to highlight urgent symptoms, accelerating clinical attention for vulnerable cases like maternal hypertension.

“The system helps frontline workers identify patients who may need earlier professional evaluation.”
Important Notice: This protocol is designed strictly for risk screening and priority triage, not diagnostic decision-making.

Feature 03 • Seamless Continuity

E-Referral & Referral ID

Prevent drop-offs when moving from rural sub-centres to specialty hospitals. Every referral is secured with a persistent ID and QR token that tracks the patient until final specialist review.

“The referral remains trackable instead of ending when the patient leaves the PHC.”

Feature 04 • Frontline Retention

Care Plan & Follow-Up

Healthcare doesn't stop after the doctor's visit. Actionable care plans automatically generate scheduled reminders for ASHA workers to monitor recovery, medication adherence, and follow-up tests.

“Care plans and reminders help ASHA workers track which patients need follow-up and when.”

Feature 05 • Zero-Wasted-Trips

Medicine & Diagnostic Availability

Rural patients frequently travel hours only to find stockouts. This feature provides ASHA workers with real-time visibility into drug inventories and diagnostic test capabilities at neighboring health centers.

“ASHA workers can identify where medicines or tests are available before sending the patient, helping reduce unnecessary travel.”
